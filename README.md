# HR-Deshboard-Power-BI-

**HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI**

**Project Overview**

This project presents an HR Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to help organizations monitor workforce metrics and make data-driven HR decisions.
The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, attrition trends, job roles, and performance indicators, enabling HR teams to identify risks and improve retention strategies.

**Objectives**
  - Analyze employee attrition and retention
  - Understand workforce distribution by age, gender, department, and job role
  - Identify key factors contributing to employee turnover
  - Support strategic HR planning through interactive visualizations

**Dataset**
Type: HR employee data

**Tools Used**
  - Power BI
  - Data cleaning and modeling
  - DAX measures for KPIs

**Key Insights**
  - Higher attrition observed in specific job roles and departments
  - Younger employees show a higher turnover rate
  - Salary and experience impact employee retention

**Conclusion**

The HR Power BI Dashboard helps HR teams quickly identify workforce trends and attrition risks, enabling proactive decision-making and effective talent management.
